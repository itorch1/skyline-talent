# Skyline Talent

Landing page for a modeling agency.
Features photos of models, about us, contacts section, and offers information about what avenues they might be attracted to

## Design

Luxurious modeling agency. Primary target audience should be beautiful women interested in social events, promoting themselves, and building social connections with wealthy men.
Intention of the business should be clear.

## Target audience

- Beautiful women interested in modeling.
  They will have photos taken of them, and the agency will organize meetups with men, go to social events, social media events, or any type of event that needs lots of beautiful women

- Rich men interested in having meetups with beautiful women: dates, social events etc

## Needs

Business: Connect both parties and receive compensation
Client: Provide information and contacts so both parties can easily understand the goal of the business and easily get in touch

## Sections

- Navigation
- Hero
- About us
- Gallery
- Steps
- Contact us (Footer)
